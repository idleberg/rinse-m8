# 77: Sort the Shows tab freshest first

**What to build:** The Shows tab can be sorted "Freshest first": shows ordered by last aired date, newest first, with shows that have no known date at the bottom. A Last aired column shows the date. A show's last aired date is the later of its seed date and its locally recorded one. Add **Show record** and **Last aired** to the glossary.

**Blocked by:** 75 (Record each show's last aired date), 76 (Backfill seed of last aired dates)

**Status:** done
