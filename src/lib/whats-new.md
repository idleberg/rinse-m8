# Automatic downloads

Subscribing to a show now just tells you when a new episode is out. Nothing gets downloaded unless you ask for it.

You choose what happens in Settings, under **Automatic downloads**:

- **Off**: nothing downloads by itself. This is the default.
- **Selected shows**: only the shows you pick download new episodes. When you subscribe to a show, you're asked whether it should download too.
- **All subscribed shows**: every show you subscribe to downloads new episodes.

With **Selected shows**, you can switch a show on or off at any time from the menu next to any of its episodes.
