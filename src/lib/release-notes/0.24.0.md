# Themes

Rinse M8 has a new look, and now lets you pick one. Choose a theme in Settings, under **Theme**:

- **Graphix**: new, and the default. Space Mono in natural casing, neutral greys instead of the warm paper, and splashes of orange, green and blue.
- **Asciitron**: the look you know. Pick it to switch back.
- more to come!

Both work in light, dark and system appearance.

Want to tweak it further? Put a `custom.css` in the app's config folder. **Custom style** in Settings opens it, and links to the parts you can restyle.
