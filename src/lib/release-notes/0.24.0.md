# Themes

Rinse M8 can now change its look. Pick a theme in Settings, under **Theme**:

- **Asciitron**: the look you know. This is the default.
- **Graphix**: new. Space Mono in natural casing, neutral greys instead of the warm paper, and splashes of orange, green and blue.

Both work in light, dark and system appearance.

Want to tweak it further? Put a `custom.css` in the app's config folder. **Custom style** in Settings opens it, and links to the parts you can restyle.
